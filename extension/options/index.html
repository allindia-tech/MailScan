PRIVACY & FORENSIC TRANSPARENCY

Zero-Trace Policy: The extension extracts visible email metadata (sender, subject, plain text body, hyperlinks, and attachment descriptors) strictly on-demand.

Credential Security: No inbox passwords, OAuth master tokens, or unrelated browser cookies are stored or accessed by the extension.

Header Transport Note: Standard webmail interfaces (Gmail/Outlook DOM) do not expose raw SMTP Received headers. The extension marks extractions accordingly and reconstructs clean RFC 5322 metadata.
